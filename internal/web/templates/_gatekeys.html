Keyboard shortcuts

Single keys work when the cursor is not in a text box — Esc leaves one.

i
Check in — the Last name box
o
Check out — the on-site list{{if eq .InProgress "out"}} (or the open check-out box){{end}}
v
{{if and (ne .GateMode "vehicle") .Cfg.VehicleModeEnabled}}Vehicle check-in page{{else}}Vehicle number box{{end}}
/
Search the on-site list
h
Home
?
Show or hide this list

In a form, Enter moves to the next box, in the same order as Tab, and stops at every question — a “Confirm who this is” ask, a warning, the final “Check in now?” — rather than answering it. On the on-site list, Up and Down move between people, Space ticks, Enter goes to the check-out box for the ticked people. After a check-in or check-out the cursor is back in the Last name box.
